The Order Parameter

Module 2: Synchronization and Collective Behaviour

To visualize the Kuramoto model, picture each phase \(\theta_j\) as a point \(e^{i\theta_j}\) running around the unit circle in the complex plane (Strogatz 2000).

Definition

The complex order parameter is the centroid of these points:

\[ r e^{i\psi} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{j=1}^N e^{i\theta_j}. \tag{1}\]

  • \(r \in [0, 1]\) is the coherence. \(r = 1\) when all phases coincide. \(r \approx 0\) when the phases are spread around the circle.
  • \(\psi\) is the mean phase, the phase of the collective rhythm.

In Figure 1 the initial phases cover only part of the circle, so the red vector has a clear length and direction. To see the vector move as the phases evolve, run the script amlab/odes_coupled/kuramoto.py. You build the same animation yourself in Animation.

Mean-field form

Multiply Equation 1 by \(e^{-i\theta_i}\) and take the imaginary part:

\[ r\sin(\psi - \theta_i) = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{j=1}^N \sin(\theta_j - \theta_i). \]

The Kuramoto model therefore becomes

\[ \dot\theta_i = \omega_i + K r \sin(\psi - \theta_i),\quad i = 1, \dots, N. \tag{2}\]

This rewriting is exact for every \(N\) (Strogatz 2000). Each oscillator no longer sees the others one by one. It sees only the mean field: it is pulled toward the mean phase \(\psi\) with effective strength \(Kr\). Computing \(r\) and \(\psi\) costs \(O(N)\) operations per step, while the double sum in the pairwise form costs \(O(N^2)\).

Feedback between coherence and coupling

Equation 2 shows why synchronization can appear spontaneously (Strogatz 2000):

  • Oscillators are pulled toward \(\psi\) with strength \(Kr\).
  • If more of them cluster, \(r\) grows.
  • A larger \(r\) increases the pull \(Kr\), which recruits more oscillators.

The loop is opposed by the spread of natural frequencies. Oscillators whose \(|\omega_i|\) exceeds \(Kr\) cannot follow the mean field and keep drifting. The balance sets a threshold coupling \(K_c\), derived in Critical Coupling.

NoteExercise

Suppose the \(N\) phases are independent and uniform on \([0, 2\pi)\). Show that \(\mathbb{E}[r^2] = 1/N\). What does this imply for the value of \(r\) you measure in an incoherent simulation?

TipSolution

\[ r^2 = \left|\frac{1}{N}\sum_j e^{i\theta_j}\right|^2 = \frac{1}{N^2}\sum_{j,k} e^{i(\theta_j - \theta_k)}. \]

The \(N\) terms with \(j = k\) equal \(1\). For \(j \ne k\) the phases are independent, so \(\mathbb{E}[e^{i(\theta_j-\theta_k)}] = \mathbb{E}[e^{i\theta_j}]\,\mathbb{E}[e^{-i\theta_k}] = 0\). Hence \(\mathbb{E}[r^2] = N/N^2 = 1/N\). An incoherent population with \(N = 100\) shows \(r\) of order \(0.1\), not \(0\). You will see this floor in Finite-Size Effects.

NoteExercise

Compute \(r\) for three oscillators at phases \(0\), \(2\pi/3\) and \(4\pi/3\), and for three oscillators at \(0\), \(0\) and \(\pi\).

TipSolution

The first three points are the cube roots of unity, which sum to zero, so \(r = 0\). For the second set, \(e^{0} + e^{0} + e^{i\pi} = 1\), so \(r = 1/3\) and \(\psi = 0\).

To visualize the Kuramoto model, picture each phase $\theta_j$ as a point $e^{i\theta_j}$ running around the unit circle in the complex plane [@strogatz2000kuramoto].

```{python}
#| label: fig-kuramoto-circle
#| fig-cap: 'Oscillators (blue dots) on the unit circle. The order parameter (red) points towards the centroid of the phases.'
#| fig-width: 5
#| fig-height: 5
#| echo: false

import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

from amlab.odes_coupled.kuramoto import initialize_oscillators, kuramoto_order_parameter

num_oscillators = 100
theta, omega = initialize_oscillators(
    num_oscillators,
    distribution="normal",
    scale_omega=0.5,
    scale_phase=0.6,
    seed=1)

x = np.cos(theta)
y = np.sin(theta)

r, phi, rcosphi, rsinphi = kuramoto_order_parameter(theta)

fig, ax = plt.subplots(figsize=(5, 5))
ax.set_xlabel(r"$\cos\theta$")
ax.set_ylabel(r"$\sin\theta$")
ax.set_xlim(-1.1, 1.1)
ax.set_ylim(-1.1, 1.1)
ax.set_aspect("equal")
ax.grid(True)
circle = plt.Circle((0, 0), 1, color="lightgray", fill=False)
ax.add_artist(circle)
ax.scatter(x, y, s=50, color="blue", alpha=0.5)
ax.plot([0, rcosphi], [0, rsinphi], color="red", linewidth=2)
ax.plot([rcosphi], [rsinphi], "ro", markersize=6)
plt.show()
```

## Definition

The complex **order parameter** is the centroid of these points:

$$
r e^{i\psi} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{j=1}^N e^{i\theta_j}.

- $r \in [0, 1]$ is the **coherence**. $r = 1$ when all phases coincide. $r \approx 0$ when the phases are spread around the circle.
- $\psi$ is the **mean phase**, the phase of the collective rhythm.
